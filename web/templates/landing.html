How it works

  1. Collect every price

    Kalshi, Polymarket and, where available, US sportsbooks, for every NBA player prop on the slate. Each read is timestamped.

  2. Remove the margin

    Every two-sided price includes the venue's cut. We remove it and take the median across venues, so one stale quote cannot move the consensus.

  3. Compare and explain

    Each line shows the offered price against that consensus, our statistical projection, and how much weight the model has earned in testing. For most markets that weight is zero today, and the page says so.

Honest about what the model can do

Most prediction products claim an edge. We publish every graded date instead. On the lines graded so far, the market consensus has been at least as accurate as our own model, so the market leads and our projection sits beside it, clearly labelled. When a gap appears, you can see whether it comes from the model or only from one venue being priced differently from the others.
