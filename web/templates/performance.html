Brier score is the mean squared error of a probability; lower is better, and 0.25 is what always guessing 50% scores. "Flat-stake return" is the average profit per $1 if every flagged positive gap had been bought at the listed price. Lines for one player and game are correlated, so a single night is a small sample.
